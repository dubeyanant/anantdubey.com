Delete your account

Deleting your Coach account is final and cannot be reverted. All of your data will be deleted within 30 days.

In the app

  1. Open Coach and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

You're signed out at once and this phone's copy is removed.

By email

No longer have the app? Write to contact@anantdubey.com from the email address on your account. Use the subject "Delete my Coach account". We'll delete it and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything in your account. That's your profile, workouts, routines, runs and their routes, food, water, body details and health data. Everything the coach knows about you goes too.

What stays

Nothing, once the 30 days are up. What Coach wrote to Health Connect stays on your phone in Health Connect. You can delete it there.
