Your training, food and recovery in one place.

Coach logs your workouts, runs and meals. It reads your watch through Health Connect. An optional AI coach plans what comes next from your own data.

Workouts

Sets, weights, reps and RPE. Routines, supersets and a rest timer. Records and progress over time.

Runs

GPS runs with splits and your route. Story cards to share. Runs from other apps count too.

Food and water

Built for Indian food. Katori sizes, Hindi spellings, vegetarian and Jain diets.

Recovery

A daily score from your sleep and heart data. It comes from an ordinary watch.

The AI coach comes with a 14-day free trial. After it, Coach Pro is ₹299 a month. A year is ₹2,499. Logging, your history and Health Connect stay free.

Join the beta

Coach is in closed testing on Google Play. You need an Android phone and a Google account. Use the same Google account for every step.

  1. Join the testers' group. Open the group and tap Join group.
    Join coach-beta
  2. Become a tester. Open Coach's testing page and tap Become a tester. If it says the page isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and open it again.
    Open the testing page
  3. Install Coach from Google Play. Open the link on your phone and tap Install. It can take a few minutes to show after step 2.
    Coach on Google Play
  4. Sign in with Google. Then log one workout, run or meal.

Keep Coach installed for 14 days and open it a few times a week. Google counts testers who stay. Testers get Coach Pro free.

Updates come through Google Play. Coach asks you to install each one when you open it.
