---
title: "I Found Myself"
date: "2024-11-18"
---

I lost myself; Or was I just someone else?
Am I the one who talks to you, or the one who speaks to myself?
A friend, a foe, a child, a parent, a lover; Soul or flesh?
Someone in the crowd, or the one who can?
Am I this soil, this air, this water, or the whole universe?
Still searching for myself in this little verse.
