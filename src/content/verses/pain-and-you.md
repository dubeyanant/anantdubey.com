---
title: "Pain & You"
date: "2022-05-25"
---

I'm sorry that I can't give you the time you
needed in this painful moment of yours,
so sorry that I'm not with you when you needed
me the most.
In this instant I am as lost
as you are, bearing the cost
of being in this alone.
I can't atone
this sin, I mourn,
and I sought
the forgiveness for my feelings are true.
In short
I want
You.
And.
Your pain.
