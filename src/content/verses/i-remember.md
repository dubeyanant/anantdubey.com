---
title: "I Remember"
date: "2023-06-03"
---

There was a drizzle,
I held your hands and ran.
I should have stayed,
and hugged you while it rained.

I should have listened,
when your eyes spoke.
It was him, not me,
now, Can you see?

I was talking;
you should have said:
'Anant, just shut up and kiss.'
Or should I have taken that risk?

Who knows what would have happened,
if I had stayed in that park with you?
You would have been me,
I would have been you.
