title: "Grass Sky & Sea"
date: "2024-03-21"
---

It's grass, sky, and sea.

Where the stars meet,  
where the birds fly,  
and I soar high.

Where innocence breathes,  
where the flowers bloom,  
and I see the moon.

Where the happiness seeds,  
where love is free,  
and that's where I want to be.

Where it's grass, sky, and sea.
